Area

Areas are boxes and cylinders placed in the course, each with a type that says what happens inside it. The game reads only four area types: camera (0), environment effect (1), moving road (3) and sound (11).

Parameters

Parameters are displayed in the entry list:

  • Shape: the shape of the area. Areas only reach upward from their position.
    • 0 - Box: 1000 * ScaleX units wide, 1000 * ScaleZ deep, from its position up to 1000 * ScaleY above it.
    • 1 - Cylinder: radius 500 * ScaleX and 500 * ScaleZ (an ellipse when they differ), up to 500 * ScaleY above its position. It has no bottom: everything below its top counts as inside, however far down.
    • Any other value makes the game skip the area, which can crash it.
  • Type: what the area does.
    • 0 - Camera: while the watched kart is inside, replays show the camera given by CAMEIndex.
    • 1 - Environment Effect: switches on one of the course slot's environment effects (falling leaves, snow, rain and the like) for the player inside. Which particles each effect slot shows is not in the course files, it depends on the course slot the course replaces.
    • 3 - Moving Road: karts driving on moving road collision (KCL type 0x0C) inside it are pushed along the route given by RouteID. The area alone does nothing, and that collision without an area does nothing either.
    • 11 - Sound: plays an ambient sound (RouteID -1 or 0), or a sound placed along a route (RouteID 1 or more), such as a river or the sea.
    • Other types are not read by the vanilla game.
  • CAMEIndex: camera areas only, the camera shown while the watched kart is inside. Cameras of type 0 or with a Video Next other than 0 are left out.
  • Priority: camera areas only, where camera areas overlap, the one with the highest priority is used; the area the kart is already in is kept while it stays inside. The other area types ignore it and use the first matching area in the list.
  • PositionXYZ: the centre of the bottom of the area.
  • RotationXYZ: rotation of the area.
  • ScaleXYZ: size of the area (see Shape).
  • Settings1: environment effect areas: the effect slot switched on, 0 to 3. Sound areas: the sound ID (ambient sound areas: 0 to 3 stop the ambient sound, 4 and more choose one. Route sound areas: the sound placed on the route).
  • Settings2: environment effect areas: a delay in seconds after the countdown starts, during which the area switches nothing on. Route sound areas: 0 places the sound at the nearest point of the route, 1 the same ignoring heights, 2 on a flat surface at the height of the first route point.
  • RouteID: moving road areas: the route the karts are pushed along (the push strength is the Speed of the route points / 100). Sound areas: -1 or 0 for an ambient sound, 1 or more for a sound along that route.
  • EnemyID: not read by the game (always -1).
  • Unknown: not read by the game, probably padding (always 0000).

Tips

  • Without at least one camera area, the course has no replay cameras and no course intro.
  • Cover the whole track with camera areas, with a little overlap. When the kart is outside every camera area for 20 frames the replay goes back to the kart camera, and comes back to the area cameras only after 60 frames inside. The Replay Camera button of the 3D view shows the camera of the area the camera kart is in.
  • Use a box where something below the area must stay out: a cylinder also covers everything below it.
  • What environment effect and sound areas do depends on the course slot the course replaces. On Neo Bowser City, DK Pass and Kalimari Desert, effect areas switch the weather off.
  • At most 10 ambient sound areas and 10 route sound areas.
