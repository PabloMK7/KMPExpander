Camera Settings

Cameras are used for the replays (chosen by the camera areas) and of the course intro. A camera is only ever shown through a camera area or the intro.

Camera types

  • 0 - Goal: built by the game for the first 5 seconds after the finish, but never shown. It orbits the kart like type 3.
  • 1 - Fixed: stands at its position (or rides its route if it has one) and looks at the kart.
  • 2 - Path: rides its route and looks at the kart.
  • 3 - Follow: orbits the kart: Viewpoint Begin X turns it around the kart from behind (degrees), Y tilts it (degrees, negative is above), Z is its distance. Viewpoint End turns its view.
  • 4 - Fixed, Moving Look-at: stands at its position and looks at a point moving from Viewpoint Begin to Viewpoint End (course coordinates).
  • 5 - Path, Moving Look-at: rides its route and looks at a point moving from Viewpoint Begin to Viewpoint End. Used by the course intro.
  • 6 - Follow Path: circles the kart: its route points are offsets from the kart (the original courses use small circles of radius 40 to 60 around (0, 0, 0)).
  • 7, 8 - Follow Path (2nd / 3rd place): as type 6, following the second or third driver of the award ceremony.

Parameters

  • Next: the next camera of the intro chain, -1 for the last one. On follow cameras (0, 3, 6, 7, 8), -2 keeps the camera level instead of tilting it with the kart.
  • VideoNext: not 0 on the pair of cameras that most likely rendered the course preview video of the menu. The game leaves such cameras out of replays and of the intro.
  • RouteID: the route the camera rides. Its speed is the Speed of the route points / 100 units per frame, blended between points.
  • FOVBegin, FOVEnd, FOVSpeed: the field of view (degrees) starts at FOV Begin and moves toward FOV End by FOV Speed hundredths of a degree per frame.
  • ViewpointBegin, ViewpointEnd, ViewpointSpeed: see the camera types. For types 4 and 5 the look-at point moves at Viewpoint Speed hundredths of a unit per frame (0 keeps it at Viewpoint Begin).
  • Duration: above 0 only on the intro cameras. The first camera with a duration (and a Video Next of 0) starts the intro chain.
  • PointSpeed, StartFlag, VideoFlag, Rotation: not read by the game.

Course intro

  • Make a chain of three cameras linked by Next (the last one -1), each with a duration above 0. Type 5 is the usual choice.
  • The shots last 232, 204 and 240 frames whatever durations you write, a fourth camera would play until the intro ends after 700 frames. For the last camera, the duration is the time its look-at point takes to reach Viewpoint End (240 matches the shot).
  • The karts only appear in the third shot, so end the intro on the start line. Nothing closer than 30 units to the camera is drawn.
  • Never link the cameras in a loop, the game would hang while loading the course. Keep the duration of the other cameras at 0.
  • Play Intro Camera plays the intro as the game does, in the 3D view it shows the camera's view on a 5:3 screen like the 3DS's (View menu: "3DS screen at 400 x 240 pixels" for its own size).

Replay cameras

  • Each camera area shows its camera while the watched kart is inside, every cut restarts the camera from the start of its route and field of view.
  • The Replay Camera button of the 3D view shows them for the camera kart of the kart simulation, while the karts drive.
